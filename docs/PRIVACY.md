# Privacy and acceptable use

Beacon is only for devices voluntarily registered by their verified owners. An IMEI is an inventory identifier, **not a public location source**. Beacon has no carrier, cell-tower, or law-enforcement access. Location exists only when the authorized companion app has permission, obtains a device location, and can reach the service.

We collect account identity, device details, pairing state, location and accuracy, timestamps, battery/connectivity telemetry, security events, and optional recovery contact text to provide recovery features. We do not sell location data. Owners can unlink devices, revoke pairing credentials, delete history, or delete their account. Administrators receive operational and abuse metadata by default—not unrestricted location access. Define retention periods and a lawful basis appropriate to the deployment jurisdiction before production use.

Background location can materially affect privacy and battery. The Android app must use an explicit disclosure and runtime consent; users can revoke permission at any time. Emergency recovery should involve local authorities rather than confronting a suspected finder or thief.
